How to study

  • The chapters follow the course schedule: read them in order, each one consolidates in a single place everything the decks of that module say about a topic.
  • Every chapter carries a lab section: the operational steps to run on your own machine, plus the R&D tasks you can pick from for the exam project.
  • The widgets (simulators, annotated code, state explorers, quizzes) are reconstructed from the lecture material: use them actively, do not just read them.
  • The plates are hand-drawn technical figures, not slide screenshots: they redraw the mechanism rather than reproduce the original picture.
  • Each section is tagged with the data-src of the deck it comes from, so you can always go back to the original slides.
  • The exam is a discussion of the tasks you completed (one large one, or several smaller ones) plus the links between them and the rest of the course, so read across parts, not just within one.
