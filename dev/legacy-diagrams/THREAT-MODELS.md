## Presentation and semantics

Two native tables separate knowledge/access from candidate-generation strategy.
Headers have row/column scope and captions. The 700px minimum width and focusable
horizontal region preserve the chapter's actual font size rather than squeezing
three prose columns into a mobile viewport. No new CSS, font or client script is
added. This comparison is better represented as a table than another flowchart.

Targeted and untargeted conditions are stated for a fixed classifier, correctly
classified clean input, fixed wrong target and valid label-preserving candidate.
The existing transfer evaluator is reused to verify the logical subset relation,
its strictness with three or more classes and equality for binary classification.
A native details example enumerates three possible predictions; it is explicitly
synthetic, not a road experiment. The existing section-6 diagram and denominator
example are linked, not duplicated or regenerated. System harm is distinguished
from a model-level success criterion.

The checklist also calls for input constraints, query/computation budgets,
eligible examples and stochastic-evaluation rules. It does not claim that larger
capabilities guarantee a successful finite run. The overviews distinguish the
lesson's two teaching threads from an exhaustive taxonomy and remove stale
promises of universal transfer, automatic CIA failures or validated deployment.
