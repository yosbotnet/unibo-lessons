## Confirmed problems

- At a=0 the canvas widget called the sample approximately symmetric because
  “mean ≈ median ≈ mode”, yet printed mean=100, median=100, “mode”=44.3.
  It selected only the first maximum-count histogram bin and treated its center
  as a unique sample mode. Every generated observation actually occurs once.
- The chapter treated equality of three summaries as a symmetry criterion and
  described the median as unaffected by extreme values and universally preferable.
- Seven-speed quartiles from the slide were presented alongside library calls
  using a different convention. It counted values equal to a quartile as strictly
  below/above it and asserted exact percentile fractions despite ties.
- Variance around the sample mean was labelled as a known-population-mean formula.
  The prose attributed a blanket n−1 default to “SciPy stats”. The CV call uses
  ddof=0, while the slide's 14.3% uses the n−1 SD. The n-denominator SD printed
  as 18.14 actually rounds to 18.15 at two decimal places.
- CV was presented as comparable across arbitrary scales, without distinguishing
  positive rescaling from a change of origin or explaining the zero-mean issue.

## Corrections and native graphics

`oa/assets/center.js` reuses the reviewed frequency/bin and box/quantile models.
It calculates source summaries, every tied sample mode, and the deterministic
121-point transformation. `center-widget.js` progressively enhances static
SVG/table markup. `oa-center-content.cjs` owns sections 3–5 and their quiz answers;
the chapter is generated by an explicit patch, never hand-adjusted coordinates.

The new histogram has 14 equal-width bins, all counts and tied highest bins in
a native table, and distinct solid-mean/dashed-median strokes. At zero their
coincidence is explicit. It calls a a transformation parameter, not a standardized
skewness statistic. Median=100 follows from monotonicity and fixing the middle
grid point, not immunity of medians to arbitrary changes. The numeric domain and
bin width vary with a; both captions and axis state that fact. No values are hidden
or discarded. With JavaScript off, the full a=0 chart/table remains available.

A second SVG has exact, synthetic finite-sample counterexamples:

- `[-4,-1,0,0,0,2,3]`: mean=median=mode=0, but not reflection-symmetric.
- `[-2,-2,-1,1,2,2]`: symmetric about zero, but modes at −2 and 2.

One dot represents each observation; multiplicities are preserved. The two panels
use the same scale. The 680px canvas removes unnecessary right padding so the
second panel can be read at the right end of the mobile scroll region. Original
font and palette tokens are shared with the earlier histogram; no smaller fonts.

The speed example is calculated from `[151,124,132,170,146,124,113]`:

Calculations use unrounded values. The table of seven deviations retains source
order and independently rounded cells/totals. The six hotel quotes and their
median 283/mode 274 remain. The separate range example (25–203) is distinguished
from the actual seven speeds (113–170).

The text distinguishes observed SS/n, unbiased SS/(n−1) under the stated i.i.d.
finite-variance model, and mean-square deviations around a known true μ. Unbiased
variance does not imply unbiased SD or valid inference for dependent observations.
Function defaults are explicit. CV interpretation is restricted to suitable
positive ratio scales; software returning negative/undefined values does not
establish a meaningful relative-spread interpretation. No Excel runtime was run.
