How to study

  • Chapters are in dependency order: read them from beginning to end. Each chapter consolidates, in one place, everything the lectures said about a topic.
  • The course is built around the autonomy → intelligence → engineering arc. Parts I–III frame and motivate agency (Chapters 1–7); Part IV makes reasoning computational (Chapters 8–10); Part V makes agents concrete with two platforms — Jade (weak agents) and Jason (intentional agents) — plus the tool landscape (Chapters 11–13); Part VI turns the student into a researcher: scientific literature, the systematic literature review, and agent explainability (Chapters 14–16).
  • The widgets (simulators, state explorers, annotated code, quizzes) are reconstructed from the original lessons: use them actively, not just as reading material.
  • Every chapter lists at the end the deck it was reconstructed from. The course material covers 19 decks (A0, M0–M11, C1–C3, S1–S3) reorganized into 16 chapters; the lab repositories referenced in the case-study chapters (Jade, Jason) are the intended hands-on follow-up.
