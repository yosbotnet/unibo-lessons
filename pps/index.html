How to study

  • The lessons follow the order of the course: quality and testing first, then functional programming and Scala, then process, then logic programming. Each lesson consolidates one slide deck or two.
  • The oral exam is on Scala, functional programming and Prolog: every lesson ends with Check your understanding, exam-style questions answered in the wording of the slides.
  • The widgets (evaluation and resolution steppers, annotated code, state explorers) are worked reconstructions of the slides' examples: step through them, do not just read them.
  • Every lesson lists the slide decks it comes from in its footer. The slides are the official source; these notes are a study aid.
