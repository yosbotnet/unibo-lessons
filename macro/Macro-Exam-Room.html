How this works

  • Sixteen questions in the official mock style, drawn from the whole course: both midterms' territory, fresh numbers throughout.
  • Real rules: 40 minutes on the clock, +1 for a right answer, −⅓ for a wrong one, 0 for a blank. You can change answers freely until you hand in.
  • No feedback until the end, exactly like the real thing. When you hand in (or the clock runs out), everything gets graded, explained, and sorted by which lesson to revisit.
  • Strategy reminder from Macro-00: answer when you can eliminate even one option; leave it blank only when a question is pure fog.

Before you start, your toolkit

  • from Macro-00 and Macro-01
    The five question patterns and the measurement formulas. Deflator tables, rate denominators, growth splitting: patterns 1 and 2 open every exam gently.
  • from Macro-02, 03, 04
    The long-run machines: the pantry (S = Y − C − G, crowding out), the arcade rule (π = money growth − output growth, then i = r + π), the bathtub (u* = s/(s+f)) and the wage-floor check.
  • from Macro-05, 06, 07
    The short-run machines: the cockpit reflex table (which curve, which way), the mattress two-frame recipe (dent, then spring-back), the escalator and the sacrifice-ratio recipe.
  • from Macro-00
    The scoring game. A blank costs nothing. A guess after one elimination is already worth it on average. Panic is the only strategy that scores negative.
