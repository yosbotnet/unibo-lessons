Life of a Launch II tells the same story as one continuous shot, narrated by the thread itself, with a real-time clock and optional sound.

How to watch

The film follows one kernel launch on your RTX 5070, vecadd<<<1024, 256>>>, and one thread inside it: element 2,690, which is thread 130 of block 10, lane 2 of warp 4. It zooms from the CPU down to that lane and back out. The captions carry the explanation; the plates show it happening. Space plays and pauses, ← → jump between scenes, j / l move five seconds.

Pause whenever a caption makes a claim, and check it against the picture. Every scene is one tab of chapter 2’s pseudocode:

What is simplified

Memory latency is shortened to 18 cycles in scene 5 so it fits on screen (on your card it is several hundred). Block completion times are jittered by hand, not measured. The scheduler picks the least recently issued ready warp, a reasonable stand-in for NVIDIA’s unpublished policy. Everything else (48 SMs, 6 blocks per SM for this kernel, 12 warps per scheduler, 32-byte sectors, 32 banks) is your card’s real configuration.
