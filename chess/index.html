How to use these notes

  • The chapters follow the book’s order: Part One takes a beginner from the rules to a first opening repertoire, the basic checkmates, draws, and simple strategy; Part Two repeats the cycle — openings, endgames, tactics, strategy — at intermediate depth.
  • The boards replace the book’s diagrams. Static boards show the point with arrows and marked squares; boards with Back / Next buttons play a line move by move. Every position and move sequence was machine-checked for legality.
  • Each chapter ends with “Check yourself”: short questions with collapsible answers. Try to answer before opening them — then go play a game and look for the same pattern.
  • This is a personal rewrite of the book in original words: every idea, verdict, tip and example is here, reorganised for study. The annotated practice links and the author’s own voice are in the original.
