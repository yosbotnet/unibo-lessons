How to study

  • The chapters are in dependency order: read them from the first to the last. Each chapter consolidates in one place everything the course materials say about a topic.
  • The thread that connects them is the reduction of overhead between a domain and its executable form: DDD removes the semantic overhead, MDD the transcription overhead, MLOps and LLMOps the operational one.
  • The exam is a discussion of a group project, not a written test. Chapter 1 lists what the project must feature, and every later chapter is a source of things you will be asked to justify about your own code.
  • The widgets (simulators, annotated code, decision tools, quizzes) are reconstructed from the lecture materials: use them actively, not only as reading.
  • Each chapter ends with a Check your understanding section and lists its sources in the footer.
